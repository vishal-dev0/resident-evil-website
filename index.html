Victor Gideon is the main antagonist of the 2026 survival horror game Resident Evil Requiem , the ninth installment of Capcom's Resident Evil franchise. He is a former employee for the Umbrella Corporation who served as a researcher for the Tyrant Project before the Corporation's bankruptcy caused by Albert Wesker.
